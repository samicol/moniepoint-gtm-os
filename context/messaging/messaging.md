# Moniepoint Messaging

Every claim traces to a research file in `context/docs/research/`. Persona buckets are from `context/icp/personas.md`. Tags: [company], [customers], [competitors], [market], [signals], [voice].

## Core message

Moniepoint gives every Nigerian business equal access to the tools to grow: reliable in-person payments, and their first loan built on their own sales. In Moniepoint's words, the goal is "financial happiness," through "Inclusion + Progress," giving SMBs "equal access to the tools they need to grow and scale." [voice]

## Message pillars

1. **Payments that always work.** Nigeria's largest merchant acquirer; around 80% of the country's POS transactions; 96% of surveyed merchants reported improved transaction security. [company], [competitors], [customers]
2. **Credit off your own sales.** Working capital and a first formal loan underwritten on transaction history: "75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint"; over $700 million lent to MSMEs in 2025. [customers]
3. **One system to run the business.** Account, POS, loans and bookkeeping together, with Moniebook unifying POS and bookkeeping for retail, hospitality and health. [company]
4. **Built for the underbanked.** Focused on the informal sector and financial inclusion; "80% of merchants saw increased business earnings." [customers], [market]

## Angle per persona

- **Champion (owner-operator / store or ops manager):** stop losing sales to cash, and run every branch from one system. [customers], [signals]
- **Economic buyer (owner / MD):** fund your next move with capital priced off your own sales, not collateral. [customers]
- **Influencer (cashier / bookkeeper / accountant):** payments and books in one place, so reconciliation stops being manual. [signals], [company]

## Words to use

- "financial happiness," "Inclusion + Progress," "financial inclusion" [voice]
- "equal access," "power the dreams of SMBs" [voice]
- "your first loan," "built on your sales," "get paid in person" [customers]
- The playful register Moniepoint uses itself (e.g. "Monie moves"). [voice]

## Words and claims to avoid

- Inventing any number, metric or figure; use only cited research figures. [CLAUDE rules]
- Em dashes in any copy. [CLAUDE rules]
- Investment or financial advice framing. [CLAUDE rules]
- Consumer-wallet positioning (that is OPay/PalmPay territory, not Moniepoint's strength). [competitors]
- Self-cited scale numbers that conflict across sources without dating them (e.g. $170bn run-rate vs $17bn monthly). [voice]
