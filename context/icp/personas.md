# Moniepoint Buyer Personas

Three personas for the MSME buying unit. Every claim traces to a file in `context/docs/research/`. Source tags: [customers], [company], [signals], [market], [competitors], [voice].

Important on "in their own words": the research holds only one direct merchant quote (Mr Collins). All other pain language is paraphrased from research narratives and clearly marked as such, not presented as verbatim voice-of-customer. Collecting real VoC quotes is an open question at the end.

For micro and small merchants the three roles often collapse into one owner-operator [customers]. The roles separate at multi-outlet businesses (Moniebook's multi-location target) [signals].

---

## 1. Champion — Owner-operator / Store or Operations Manager

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | Business owner, shop owner, market trader, store manager, operations manager | [customers], [signals] |
| Measured on | Keeping sales flowing (payments that work), stock availability, daily takings and reconciliation | [customers], [signals] |

**Top three pains (paraphrased from research, not verbatim):**
1. Cash-heavy trade is risky and hard to reconcile. [signals], [customers]
2. Cannot get credit to restock or expand through normal channels; most have never had a formal loan. [customers]
3. At multiple outlets, payments and books are fragmented across locations. [signals]

Only verified direct quote available: Mr Collins (Onitsha), "Moniepoint isn't scared to support me." [customers]

**What makes them start looking:** a cash-security or reconciliation scare; needing working capital to restock or renovate (e.g. Oluremikun's loan-funded expansion); opening or running more than one outlet; a market-wide shift to digital payments (Onitsha). [customers], [signals]

**Objections (traceable to research facts):**
- POS onboarding may require funding the account first (one source cites a ₦21,500 minimum). [company]
- Published pricing is inconsistent, so cost is unclear up front. [company]

## 2. Economic buyer — Owner / Managing Director

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | Owner, proprietor, managing director; at larger merchants a finance lead (CFO-type role rare at this size) | [customers], [signals] |
| Measured on | Business growth and earnings, survival and expansion of the business | [customers] |

**Top three pains (paraphrased from research, not verbatim):**
1. Growth is capped by lack of access to affordable credit. [customers]
2. Needs the business to be more secure and resilient (security and continuity). [customers]
3. Rising regulatory pressure means the payments partner must stay compliant. [market], [signals]

**What makes them start looking:** a concrete growth goal needing capital (expansion, renovation, restocking); evidence that credit lifts earnings ("80% of merchants saw increased business earnings"); a compliance deadline that forces a partner review (data localisation Jan 2027, POS geo-tagging from Apr 2026). [customers], [market], [signals]

**Objections (traceable to research facts):**
- Trust in a fast-growing fintech's stability, given publicly conflicting growth numbers. [company], [voice]
- Regulatory uncertainty around fintechs (CBN market-structure caps and divestment rules) raises "will my provider still look like this next year" doubt. [market]

## 3. Influencer — Cashier / Bookkeeper / Accountant

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Job titles | Cashier, shop assistant, bookkeeper, accountant, family member helping run the shop | [signals], [customers] |
| Measured on | Accurate daily reconciliation, clean books, smooth checkout, fewer payment errors | [signals] |

**Top three pains (paraphrased from research, not verbatim):**
1. Reconciling cash and payments by hand is slow and error-prone. [signals]
2. No single system ties payments to bookkeeping (the gap Moniebook targets). [signals], [company]
3. Transaction security and dispute handling at the counter. [customers]

**What makes them start looking:** being handed multi-location bookkeeping that manual methods cannot keep up with; the business adopting a POS + bookkeeping tool; wanting the transaction history that later unlocks loans. [signals], [customers]

**Objections (traceable to research facts):**
- Learning and switching to a new POS + bookkeeping system (change cost). INFERRED from Moniebook being a new all-in-one system. [signals]
- Ongoing software cost at the counter (Moniebook tiers: ₦6,000 / ₦8,500 per month). [company]
