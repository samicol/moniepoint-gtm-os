# Moniepoint Positioning

Every claim traces to a research file in `context/docs/research/`. Tags: [company], [customers], [competitors], [market], [signals], [voice].

## Category

A business payments and banking platform for MSMEs: merchant acquiring and agency banking, with credit and bookkeeping attached. Moniepoint calls itself "a global business payments and banking platform." [company], [voice]

## Target customer

Nigerian MSMEs and informal-sector merchants: retail, hospitality, health, market traders, corner shops and supermarkets. Nigeria is roughly 90% of the business. [customers], [company]

## The alternative they use today

- Cash, in a cash-heavy trade environment. [signals], [customers]
- Consumer-led fintechs for payments: OPay, PalmPay. [competitors]
- Other business/payment players: Paga (payments, deposits, credit), Flutterwave (B2B and cross-border), Kuda (digital bank). [competitors]
- Traditional banks, now pushing into POS (e.g. GTCO). [competitors]

## Unique value

- Nigeria's largest merchant acquirer; a co-founder says it processes "over 80% of the POS transactions in the country." [company], [competitors]
- Owns in-person/in-store payments: "more than 2m businesses are using its payment devices" and "more than 4m account-holders"; said to "move the most money." [competitors]
- Credit underwritten off the merchant's own transaction history, reaching the underbanked: first-time formal credit for most borrowers. [customers]

## Proof

- "the partner of choice for over 1,500,000+ businesses of all sizes." [voice]
- "75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint"; more than $700 million in MSME loans in 2025. [customers]
- "powers two out of three in-person payments" in Onitsha, West Africa's largest market. [customers]
- Unicorn, $1bn+ valuation, backed by Visa and Google. [company]

## Positioning statement

For Nigerian MSMEs and informal-sector merchants who run on cash and have never had access to formal credit, Moniepoint is the business payments and banking platform that gets paid in person reliably and turns a merchant's own sales into their first working-capital loan. Unlike consumer-first fintechs or traditional banks, Moniepoint is the country's largest merchant acquirer, built for the underbanked, with payments, POS, credit and bookkeeping in one place. The proof is 1.5 million-plus businesses, around 80% of the country's POS transactions, and over $700 million lent to MSMEs in 2025, three quarters of them borrowing formally for the first time.
