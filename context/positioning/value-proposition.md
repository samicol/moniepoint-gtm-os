# Moniepoint Value Proposition

Per persona. Persona buckets are from `context/icp/personas.md`; every claim below traces to a research file in `context/docs/research/`. Tags: [company], [customers], [competitors], [market], [signals], [voice].

## Champion (owner-operator / store or ops manager)

- **Job to be done:** keep sales flowing and stock moving, across one shop or several branches. [customers]
- **Pain:** cash-heavy trade is risky and hard to reconcile; at multiple outlets, payments and books are fragmented across locations. [signals], [customers]
- **Gain:** accept card and transfer reliably, run it from one app, and (with Moniebook) unify POS and bookkeeping across branches. [company], [signals]
- **Proof:** Moniepoint "powers two out of three in-person payments" in Onitsha, West Africa's largest market; 96% of surveyed merchants said transaction security improved. [customers]

## Economic buyer (owner / MD)

- **Job to be done:** grow the business and fund expansion or restocking. [customers]
- **Pain:** growth is capped by lack of access to affordable credit; most merchants have never had a formal loan. [customers]
- **Gain:** working capital priced off their own sales, and a first formal business loan. [customers]
- **Proof:** "75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint"; "80% of merchants saw increased business earnings"; more than $700 million disbursed to MSMEs in 2025. [customers]

## Influencer (cashier / bookkeeper / accountant)

- **Job to be done:** close out the day's money accurately and keep clean books. [signals]
- **Pain:** reconciling cash and payments by hand is slow and error-prone; no single system ties payments to bookkeeping. [signals], [company]
- **Gain:** one all-in-one system for payments and bookkeeping (Moniebook). [company]
- **Proof:** Moniebook is positioned as an all-in-one POS and bookkeeping system for retail, hospitality and health businesses. [company]

## Note

The research holds only one verbatim merchant quote (Mr Collins, "Moniepoint isn't scared to support me" [customers]); the pains above are paraphrased from research narratives, not presented as customer quotes.
