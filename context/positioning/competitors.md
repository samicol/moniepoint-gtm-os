# Moniepoint Competitive Positioning

Drawn only from `context/docs/research/competitors.md` (and where noted, other research files). Scale figures come from different periods and sources; treat as approximate. Tags: [competitors], [company], [customers], [market].

## The main competitors

| Competitor | Positioning | Scale (as reported) | Overlap with Moniepoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPay | Consumer payments / mobile money | 60 million+ users (one source); 35 million (another) | Agent/POS banking and informal sector [competitors] |
| PalmPay | Consumer payments and bills | 35 million users, 15 million daily transactions | Consumer payments, some agent overlap [competitors] |
| Kuda | Digital bank (consumer-led) | Budgeting, categorization, analytics; same microfinance-bank licence upgrade | Banking, less so in-store POS [competitors] |
| Flutterwave | B2B and cross-border payments | "Africa's most valuable fintech," $3 billion valuation | Online/B2B payments (vs Monnify) [competitors] |
| Paga | Payments, moving into deposits and credit | Profitable; over ₦23 trillion processed since launch | Payments and credit [competitors] |
| Traditional banks (e.g. GTCO) | Incumbent banks entering POS | GTCO plans 200,000 POS terminals | In-store POS / merchant acquiring [competitors] |

All six, with Moniepoint, were designated by the CBN as "systemically relevant participants." [competitors]

## Where Moniepoint wins

- In-person / in-store payments and merchant acquiring: "more than 2m businesses" on its devices, "more than 4m account-holders," around 80% of the country's POS transactions, and said to "move the most money." [competitors]
- First-time credit off transaction history for the underbanked: "75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint." [customers]
- Informal-sector distribution and density (e.g. two of three in-person payments in Onitsha). [customers]

## Where Moniepoint loses

- Consumer mobile money: OPay and PalmPay have far larger consumer user bases. [competitors]
- B2B and cross-border payments brand: Flutterwave is the most valuable fintech at $3bn. [competitors]
- Profitability narrative: Paga is cited as profitable. [competitors]

## How to respond

- Lead with merchant acquiring and in-person reliability plus first-time credit, the areas Moniepoint owns, not consumer wallets. [competitors], [customers]
- For online merchants, position Monnify against gateway rivals (Flutterwave; Paystack as a rival is INFERRED in the research). [competitors]
- Use informal-market density (Onitsha-type) as proof no consumer-first rival can match. [customers]
- Where banks push into POS, lean on scale and credit-off-sales rather than competing on being a bank. [competitors]
