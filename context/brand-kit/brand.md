# Moniepoint Brand: Tone and Key Phrases

From Moniepoint's own copy, as captured in `context/docs/research/voice.md`. Quotes are verbatim. Tag: [voice] unless noted.

## Tone of voice

- **Mission-driven and warm:** everything ladders to "financial happiness" and inclusion. [voice]
- **Plain and benefit-led:** "a global business payments and banking platform"; "equal access to the tools they need to grow and scale." [voice]
- **Playful and pun-forward** around the "Monie / financial happiness" theme, especially in microcopy. [voice]
- **Problem-solving, not hype:** a VP frames it as "solving real problems and creating sustainable solutions." [voice]

## Key phrases (verbatim)

Mission and north star:
- "to create a society where everyone experiences financial happiness"
- "We are creating financial happiness for every African, everywhere"
- "Inclusion + Progress = Financial happiness"

What they call themselves:
- "a global business payments and banking platform"
- "power the dreams of SMBs"
- "the leading agency banking solution in Nigeria"
- "Nigeria's strongest distribution network for financial services"

Playful register (impact-site microcopy):
- "First time's the charm, if you ask us."
- "Talk about making Monie moves!"
- "They're officially in a league of their own."
- "Who knew financial happiness would have a literal impact? (We did.)"

Executive voice:
- "Our Focus Is on Solving Real Problems and Creating Sustainable Solutions."

## House rules for anyone writing in this voice

- No em dashes. [CLAUDE rules]
- Cite a source for every company fact; never invent numbers. [CLAUDE rules]
- Self-cited scale numbers conflict across sources ($170bn run-rate vs $17bn monthly); date any figure used. [voice]
