## Aggregate impact (self-published, not per-customer)

- 75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint; 80% of merchants saw increased business earnings; 85% feel more confident about financial goals; 83% report improved quality of life. Source: customers.md, impact.moniepoint.com
- More than $700 million in MSME loans in 2025; three of four merchants received formal business credit for the first time. Source: customers.md, guardian.ng
- Onitsha: "powers two out of three in-person payments." Source: customers.md, moniepoint.com/blog
