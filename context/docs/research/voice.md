# Moniepoint: Voice (how they describe themselves, in their own words)

Exact self-description language. Every quote carries a source URL and the date checked. Quotes are verbatim. INFERRED marks reasoning. "Not found" marks unknowns.

NOTE: moniepoint.com/about returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch. The verbatim lines below are from an official-content mirror (moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us) and from the live impact site (impact.moniepoint.com). Re-confirm against moniepoint.com in a browser before using in external copy.

All items checked on 2026-11-17.

## Mission / north star

- "to create a society where everyone experiences financial happiness" Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- "We are creating financial happiness for every African, everywhere" Source: https://impact.moniepoint.com/
- Their stated "formula": "Inclusion + Progress = Financial happiness" Source: https://impact.moniepoint.com/
- A blog frames the goal as "a world where every African can experience financial happiness," treating access to financial services as the starting point. Source: https://moniepoint.com/blog/how-weve-built-a-bank-in-every-neighbourhood

## What they call themselves

- "a global business payments and banking platform" Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- They "power the dreams of SMBs" and aim to give small businesses "equal access to the tools they need to grow and scale." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- "a fully remote tech company with a diverse workforce worldwide," "headquartered in London, with offices in the US, Nairobi and Lagos." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us

## Product descriptions (their words)

- Moniepoint: "the leading agency banking solution in Nigeria"; basis of "Nigeria's strongest distribution network for financial services." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- Monnify: a product for merchants "selling goods and services who needed to receive payments online." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- 2022 milestone: "We received a banking licence and began offering banking services to businesses across the country." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
