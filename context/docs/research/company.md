# Moniepoint: Company

Raw research. Every fact carries a source URL and the date it was checked. Quotes are the company's or source's exact words. Items I reasoned to rather than read are marked INFERRED. Unknowns are marked "Not found."

All items checked on 2026-11-17 unless noted.

## What they are

- "a global business payments and banking platform" whose mission is "to create a society where everyone experiences financial happiness." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us (official-content mirror of moniepoint.com/about, which returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch)
- Began in 2015 as TeamApt Inc., a bank-technology vendor that licensed software to Nigerian banks; rebranded to Moniepoint in 2023. Source: https://techcabal.com/profile/tosin-eniolorunda/ and https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- "Deployed to 95% of Nigerian banks" at the infrastructure stage, serving over 10 million of their customers. Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- Described by TechCrunch as "Nigeria's largest merchant acquirer," powering most of the country's point-of-sale (POS) transactions. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/

## What they sell (products)

- **Business banking / account:** open a business account, accept online and offline payments, pay bills, request expense cards, apply for business loans. Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moniepoint/id1661134941
- **POS terminals / agency banking:** described on the company site as "the leading agency banking solution in Nigeria" and "Nigeria's strongest distribution network for financial services." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- **Monnify (payment gateway):** product for merchants "selling goods and services who needed to receive payments online." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- **Moniebook:** all-in-one POS and bookkeeping software for retail, hospitality and health businesses, launched 2026. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/moniepoint-launches-moniebook-for-msmes-bookkeeping
- **Credit / loans:** working capital, business expansion loans, business payment cards, accounting and bookkeeping tools, and insurance for SMBs. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- **Personal banking:** entered personal/consumer banking recently, reporting "20x customer growth over the past year" in that push. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- **MonieWorld (UK-Nigeria remittance / diaspora banking):** launched April 2025. Source: https://msmeafricaonline.com/moniepoint-enters-diaspora-banking-with-monieworld-targeting-uk-nigeria-remittance-market — NOTE: reported in August 2026 to be winding down, with resources redirected to core African markets. This wind-down is reported secondhand in search coverage (techeconomy MonieWorld coverage, https://techeconomy.ng/moniepoint-launches-monieworld and related) and I could not open a primary confirmation. Treat as UNVERIFIED pending a primary source.

## Pricing model

Published pricing is partial and sources conflict; treat as indicative, not authoritative.

- **Moniebook:** Core tier ₦6,000/month (small businesses); Pro tier ₦8,500/month (multi-location). Optional add-ons for extra registers, branches, implementation support. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/moniepoint-launches-moniebook-for-msmes-bookkeeping
- **Monnify gateway:** "no setup fees," no monthly charges, pay only on successful transactions; a 1.5% bank transfer fee capped at ₦2,000 is listed. Source: https://monnify.com/pricing.html
- **POS terminal onboarding:** sources conflict; one says no upfront registration cost, another says a minimum funding of ₦21,500 in the new business account is required to receive a POS. Source: https://nairacompare.ng/blogs/reviews/moniepoint-review
- Full current rates: moniepoint.com/ng/business (could not auto-fetch; 403).

## Markets

- Nigeria is the core market. Per TechCrunch, "Nigeria accounts for 90% of its business." Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- Company describes itself as "headquartered in London, with offices in the US, Nairobi and Lagos." Listed addresses: London (Southwark Bridge Road), Lewes, Delaware, and Lagos (Victoria Island). Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- UK entry via acquisition of Bancom Europe (completed July 2025) to secure a licence and operate across Europe without separate per-country licences. Source: https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/10/07/moniepoint-faces-heavy-losses-after-acquiring-bancom-europe-to-secure-uk-licence/
- Also expanded reported into Kenya (Nairobi office). Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us

## Funding

- **Series C (Oct 2024):** $110 million, led by Development Partners International's African Development Partners (ADP) III fund; new investor Google's Africa Investment Fund; also Verod Capital and existing investor Lightrock. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- **Valuation:** the round made Moniepoint a unicorn, valued at $1 billion or more (per Financial Times, cited by TechCrunch). Prior valuation "slightly over $800 million" in a QED-led round two years earlier. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/ and https://techcabal.com/2024/10/29/moniepoint-1bn-valuation-funding-round/
- **Series C extension (Oct 2025):** additional $90 million, bringing the round to $200 million total; company said it sits "well above the $1 billion mark" but disclosed no new valuation. Reported investors in the extension included Visa, DPI, LeapFrog Investments, and Google's Africa Investment Fund. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2025/10/21/moniepoint-raises-additional-90-million-to-close-200-million-series-c/
- **Total raised:** "Over $180 million since its 2015 launch" (as of Oct 2024). Other listed backers: QED Investors, British International Investment (BII), Endeavor Catalyst. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
