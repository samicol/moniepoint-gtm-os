# Moniepoint: Market and Regulation

Raw research on regulation and market changes that create urgency for Moniepoint's buyers (Nigerian merchants and fintechs). Every fact carries a source URL and the date checked. Quotes are exact. INFERRED marks reasoning. "Not found" marks unknowns. Several sources are law-firm or vendor blogs; dates and deadlines should be checked against the original CBN circulars before relying on them.

All items checked on 2026-11-17.

## CBN market-structure and data rules (2026-2027)

- **Data localisation:** A CBN circular (dated June 2026) requires payment data generated in Nigeria to be stored domestically. Reported effective date for full data-localisation compliance is January 1, 2027. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/06/15/cbn-mandates-banks-fintechs-to-store-payment-data-in-nigeria/
- **Market-structure / dominance caps:** The circular sets a 25% market-share threshold and a 15% cross-market cap, with compliance due by December 31, 2026; groups with integrated operations face an eight-month window to unwind or divest. Source: https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/06/16/nigerias-central-bank-orders-fintechs-to-split-their-payments-empires-or-divest/ and https://globallawexperts.com/news/nigeria-2026-fintech-rulebook-compliance-clock
  - Urgency for buyers: INFERRED. Large fintechs (including Moniepoint) may have to restructure; this can change which provider a merchant can rely on and reshape the competitive field.

## CBN AML baseline standards (2026-2028)

- March 2026 circular set staged deadlines. All regulated institutions (fintechs, PSPs, mobile money operators) had to submit a formal AML compliance roadmap to the CBN by June 10 (2026). Full compliance due by March 2028. Source: https://dojah.io/blog/cbn-aml-baseline-standards-fintechs-2026 and https://fintech.global/?p=239381
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure:** CBN ordered banks and fintechs to disclose beneficial owners as part of new compliance requirements. Source: https://arbiterz.com/?p=67289

## POS agent rules

- From April 2026, POS agents reported to operate under "tightened geo-tagging requirements, stricter location compliance rules, and renewed enforcement of exclusivity." Source: https://techpoint.africa/guide/cbn-fintech-regulations/
  - Urgency for buyers: INFERRED. Affects the agency-banking network Moniepoint and rivals run; compliance burden falls on agents/merchants.

## Financial-inclusion push

- CBN issued "tough new rules for Opay, Moniepoint, PalmPay to deepen financial inclusion." Source: https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/industry/1720462-cbn-unveils-tough-rules-opay-moniepoint-palmpay-deepen-financial-inclusion/

## Market size / context

- CBN put total POS transaction value at ₦223.27 trillion ($161.03 billion) in 2025; Nigeria had 5.90 million active POS terminals as of March 2025. Source: https://www.cbn.gov.ng (figures surfaced via search of CBN data; verify on CBN site directly). As surfaced in: https://techcabal.com/?p=180023
- Nigeria's cash-scarcity episodes and the 2023 naira redesign accelerated POS and digital-payment adoption.
