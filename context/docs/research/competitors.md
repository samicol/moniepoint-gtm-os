# Moniepoint: Competitors

Raw research. Every fact carries a source URL and the date it was checked. Quotes are exact. INFERRED marks reasoning. "Not found" marks unknowns. User counts and valuations come from different periods (2025 to mid-2026) and different metrics; treat as approximate.

All items checked on 2026-11-17.

## Moniepoint's own position (for contrast)

- Described as owning in-store payments: "more than 2m businesses are using its payment devices and it has more than 4m account-holders"; said to "move the most money." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Co-founder claim: processes "over 80% of the POS transactions in the country." Source: https://techcabal.com/2025/07/21/the-biggest-fintech-companies-in-nigeria-2025/

## Direct competitors (same buyers: Nigerian merchants / consumers / businesses)

### OPay
- Positioning: mostly consumer mobile-money and payments; dominant in consumer segment. Analysts note Moniepoint "is following a similar path to that of its rival OPay" in the informal sector. Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Scale (conflicting): "60 million+ users" in one source; "35 million individual customers" in another. Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Also a direct rival in agent/POS banking. INFERRED from overlapping agency-banking coverage.

### PalmPay
- Positioning: consumer payments and bills. Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Scale: credited with "35 million users and 15 million daily transactions." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory

### Kuda
- Positioning: digital bank (consumer-led); "offers budgeting tools, transaction categorization, and spending analytics." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Received a licence upgrade (January) to a national microfinance bank, "the same kind of upgrade Moniepoint received." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory

### Flutterwave
- Positioning: B2B and cross-border payments. "Flutterwave remains Africa's most valuable fintech company with a $3 billion valuation." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Overlap with Moniepoint is mainly B2B payments/gateway (Monnify), less so in-store POS. INFERRED.

### Paga
- Positioning: payments company that "has moved into deposits and credit." "It's profitable, privately owned, and has processed over ₦23 trillion in transactions since its launch." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory

## Adjacent / emerging competitive pressure

- **Traditional banks moving into POS:** GTCO (GTBank parent) "plans to challenge fintechs with 200,000 PoS terminals." Source: https://techcabal.com/?p=191890 (TechCabal, surfaced via search; verify exact URL)
- **Monnify-level gateway rivals (B2B online payments):** Flutterwave, Paystack. Paystack named among top Nigerian fintechs. Source: https://techcabal.com/2026/06/16/fintech-businesses-in-nigeria/ — NOTE: Paystack mention is INFERRED as a Monnify competitor from general fintech coverage; confirm positioning before using.

## Shared regulatory status (signals they are peers)

- In January, the Central Bank of Nigeria "designated the leading fintech companies, including Flutterwave, Paga, Opay, Moniepoint, Kuda and PalmPay as 'systemically relevant participants.'" Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
