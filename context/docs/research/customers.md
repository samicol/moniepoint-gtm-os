# Moniepoint: Customers

Raw research. Every fact carries a source URL and the date it was checked. Quotes are exact. INFERRED marks reasoning. "Not found" marks unknowns. Nearly all customer evidence below is Moniepoint's own marketing or self-published reports; treat figures as company-sourced and unverified independently.

All items checked on 2026-11-17.

## Who they serve

- Micro, small and medium businesses (MSMEs/SMBs) in Nigeria: retail, hospitality, health, market traders, corner shops and supermarkets. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/ and https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/01/29/from-payments-to-credit-nigerian-fintech-moniepoint-deploys-720m-to-corner-shops-and-supermarkets/
- Plus personal/consumer banking customers (recent push). Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/
- Company self-describes as "the partner of choice for over 1,500,000+ businesses of all sizes." Source: https://moniepoint-global-website.pages.dev/about-us
- TechCrunch (Oct 2024) and other coverage cite "over 2 million businesses" using its payment devices. Source: https://www.casestudies.com/company/confluent/case-study/transforming-financial-services-for-over-2-million-businesses-in-africa-with-confluent

## Company sizes / industries

- Primarily micro and small merchants and informal-sector traders; "by focusing its development strategy on the informal sector." Source: https://african.business/2026/05/long-reads/nigerian-fintechs-march-into-banks-territory
- Named industry verticals in Moniebook launch: retail, hospitality, health. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/moniepoint-launches-moniebook-for-msmes-bookkeeping
- Enterprise/large-account customers: Not found (positioning is SMB/MSME-led).

## Impact figures (self-published 2025 Impact Report)

From https://impact.moniepoint.com/ :
- "75% of entrepreneurs received their first-ever loan from Moniepoint."
- "80% of merchants saw increased business earnings."
- "85% of users feel more confident about achieving their financial goals."
- "83% of our users are experiencing improved quality of life."

From press coverage of Moniepoint's 2025 reports:
- Disbursed more than $700 million in loans to MSMEs in 2025; three of every four merchants received formal business credit for the first time. Source: https://guardian.ng/?p=2884068
- Merchants on the platform employed more than eight million people in 2025. Source: https://techcabal.com/2026/06/16/fintech-businesses-in-nigeria/ (as surfaced; verify)
- Other surveyed figures (differ by report/definition): average 36% increase in transaction value; 88% reported business growth; 91% improved operations; 96% improved transaction security. Source: https://techeconomy.ng/beyond-payments-how-moniepoint-powered-nigerias-msme-growth-in-2025
